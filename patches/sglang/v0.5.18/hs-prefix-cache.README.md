Producer ownership ends when refs may be visible in the durable stream. Successful terminal cleanup requires both joined-producer EOF and successful Consumer drain, and deletes only exact published attempt keys. Failure or timeout does not authorize reclaiming handed-off objects. Delayed callbacks cannot target a later same-sample attempt.

Ready samples share batched sink publication. After sink drain, the cache may adopt the complete output allocation without a second CPU fill copy; chunk views share that owner. Budgets charge the full unique underlying allocation, including prefix and tail, and group eviction respects every chunk lease. Cache READY is delayed until this ownership transfer. Fixed pending-batch limits are bypassed only where controller byte budgets already bound the jobs.
